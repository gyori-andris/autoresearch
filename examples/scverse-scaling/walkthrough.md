## Request

> Determine whether Scanpy sparse scaling has an actionable native bottleneck before launching optimisation. Preserve upstream behaviour and use a locked single-core profile to choose the editable surface.

## Setup

The known baseline is a one-core, eight-million-entry kernel: 12.032 ms across 30 samples. At audit the source matched upstream and all 178 ledger rows were baseline or smoke measurements. They were not 178 optimisation candidates.

## Change

A native-loop improvement is a hypothesis to investigate with profiling, not a demonstrated opportunity. Establish its share of public runtime and memory traffic before selecting unrolling, layout or instructions.

## Evaluation

The profiling gate remained stopped. A focused rerun could not obtain the benchmark lease because another worker held it. The next step is a native sampling profile under an exclusive benchmark lease.

## Result

**Not enrolled.** The baseline exists, but profiling has not qualified a bottleneck and no candidate has been measured.

## Follow-up

Obtain an exclusive benchmark lease and a native sampling profile. Establish the loop's share of public runtime before selecting an implementation change.
